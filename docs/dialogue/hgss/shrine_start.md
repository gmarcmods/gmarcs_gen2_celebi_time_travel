# HGSS — the shrine start (present day)

Transcribed from a player's real capture (GameFAQs, "Celebi Event NA Version
Script", PSI_Ground, Apr 2010) — verbatim with the poster's original typos
kept and marked. Staging comments in the transcript are reproduced as
`--narration--` rows.

Staging: examine the **Ilex Forest shrine** with an event (Fateful
Encounter) Celebi registered as your **follower**; the transcript says a
hack-with-shiny Celebi also works for testing (the event's Celebi visual
turns green if shiny — see beat 4). The trigger fires on the shrine read
itself.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | —(narration) | *Celebi jumps around, Lyra comes, her theme plays* | on shrine read |
| 2 | Lyra | `Hi, *NAME*. Have you heard the legend of the shrine?` | on approach |
| 3 | Lyra | `They say people disappear when they tamper with it.` | box 2 |
| 4 | —(narration) | *Light Flashes, Lyra is startled and comes over, Normal cave music starts* | the "cave music" before the jump is the to-be-Route-22 ambience creeping in |
| 5 | Lyra | `...What was that?` | box |
| 6 | —(narration) | *Celebi flashes, flies in circles. Interestingly, if it is hacked and Shiny, it turns Green during this* | swirl lead-in |

Notes:

- `*NAME*` is the player's name substituted by the game.
- In HGSS the man/Giovanni reveal happens later; here he is only "a light" —
  nothing revealed at the shrine.
- The shrine in HGSS is *not consumed* by this event; the trigger simply
  never re-fires after completion.
